## dive-00 — digiVance world

Begin in a calm wide view of the complete connected digiVance miniature world. Make a slow, confident forward drift along the central path toward the open studio table, with subtle parallax between the architecture, timber yard, print atelier, and textile rack. Let the single signal-lime sphere catch a soft highlight as the camera settles. Keep the four pathways readable and finish on a stable centered frame that still contains the whole world.
